Sterilization Settings

Standard Autoclave: 121°C for 15 minutes at 15 psi.

⚠️ CRITICAL: Add Antibiotics AFTER autoclaving when media cools to ~50°C.

🥔 PDA Manual Prep Steps:

  1. Wash and slice unpeeled potatoes.
  2. Boil in ~500mL distilled water for 30 mins until soft.
  3. Filter through cheesecloth; retain the liquid (Infusion).
  4. Add Agar & Dextrose to the warm infusion.
  5. Top up with distilled water to reach the final required volume.
